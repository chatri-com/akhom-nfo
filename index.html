About Me

Dr. Akhom Khatfan is a dedicated lecturer and researcher at the Faculty of Science and Fisheries Technology, RMUTSV, Songkhla, Thailand. His work focuses on aquatic plants, particularly lotus species (Nelumbo nucifera), as well as ecology, pollination biology, and environmental science. His research combines fieldwork, laboratory experiments, and genetic studies to advance understanding of tropical plant biodiversity.

Research Interests & Publications

  • Aquatic plants, especially Nelumbo nucifera (lotus species)
  • Pollination biology, seed setting, and tropical plant ecology
  • Genetic diversity, classification, and phylogenetic analysis of plants
  • Selected publications:
    • Pollen viability, germination, and seed setting of Nelumbo nucifera
    • Phylogenetic relationships between sacred lotus and woody plants
    • Genetic diversity and classification of lotus germplasm
